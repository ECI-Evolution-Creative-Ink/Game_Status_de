About the Project

Evolution Creative Ink is currently developing an entirely new multiplayer game inspired by the creativity and social experience of games such as Rec Room.


While the project takes inspiration from social gaming, all systems, mechanics and worlds are being designed independently with our own vision.


The project is currently being developed by two developers: @Knurriger_Tyler and Umbra_rr.
